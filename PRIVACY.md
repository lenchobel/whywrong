# WhyWrong privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

WhyWrong is a study app that runs inside Anna. This page explains what happens to what you type into it.

## What the app uses
- **The question you paste, the answer you picked, and the right answer (if you add it).** These are sent to Anna's AI so it can write the feedback and the practice questions. Nothing else is sent.
- **Your notebook.** When you tap "Save to notebook", the question, your answer, and the feedback are saved in your Anna account using Anna's storage. Only your account can open it in the app.

## What the app does not do
- It does not collect your name, email, location, or contacts.
- It has no ads and no analytics, and it does not sell or share your data.
- It does not use cookies or trackers of its own.
- The developer does not run a server for this app and does not receive your questions or your notebook.

## Your choices
- Delete any saved mistake from the Notebook tab with the Delete button.
- Copy your notebook out at any time with "Copy notebook as text".
- Anna handles your account and how the AI processes what you send. Anna's own terms and privacy policy apply to that, including any age requirements for using Anna.

## Please don't paste
Personal details such as your full name, address, phone number, or passwords. WhyWrong only needs the question text.

## AI can be wrong
The explanations and practice questions are written by AI. Check them against your textbook or ask your teacher if something looks off.
